"Lorcana's Hyperia City Streeted Two Days Ago -- the Presale Premium We Flagged Last Month Already Collapsed"

✨ Disney Lorcana 2026-10-25

Back in September, Hyperia City (Set 14) was still a month from release and trading on nothing but presale sealed product -- no singles, thin volume, and a booster box already running more than double MSRP. The set's local prerelease hit October 16 and wide retail followed October 23, so as of this writing there are two days of real singles and real sealed sales to check those presale numbers against. Most of them were too high, and the set also quietly debuted a rarity tier above Enchanted that nobody priced in advance.

The box and pack premium we flagged in September deflated almost exactly the way we said it would. A booster box is down 33.4% from its presale price now that real retail supply is actually on shelves, and the single booster pack fell even harder, down 37.3%. That's the predictable pattern: presale sealed product trades on scarcity, and scarcity disappears the moment prerelease events and wide release start putting real boxes into circulation. The Trove tracked the same way, down 35.0% from its presale 2.4x-MSRP peak to a more sane $76.91 -- still over MSRP, but nowhere near where it was trading blind.

The one SKU that didn't fall is the one that tells you where the thin volume actually is. Illumineer's Trove Cases are trading at $404.55, essentially unchanged from their $399.64 presale price, while every other sealed SKU dropped 27-37%. That flips the per-unit case math: a Trove Case now works out to $101.14 per Trove, more than buying a single Trove at $76.91 -- backwards from presale, when the case was a real 15.5% discount per unit. Booster box cases show the same shift in miniature: $155.23 per box in the case versus $150.45 buying singly. Read plainly, single boxes and Troves are now trading in real volume from wide retail, but case-level lots are still mostly changing hands among the same thin group of distributors that traded them presale -- case prices just haven't caught up to the repricing everything else just went through.

Hyperia City's real headline is a new rarity tier that sits above Enchanted, and it debuted at a wild internal spread. TCGplayer's own listings call it "Iconic," and there are exactly two of them in the set -- Mickey Mouse at card 241/204 and Cinderella at 242/204, both numbered past the 204-card base set the same way Enchanteds are. Mickey is pricing at $2,400.98, better than double Cinderella's $1,019.00, a 2.36x spread between the only two cards that exist in the tier. In a brand-new rarity with this little trade history, that gap is as much about which card collectors decided to chase first as it is about genuine pull-rate scarcity -- there simply aren't enough recorded sales yet to say the ratio is settled.

The Coco crossover drove the presale hype, but it isn't the chase tier once real cards started changing hands. September's presale piece pointed to the Coco pairing -- Miguel and Hector Rivera getting Enchanted treatment -- as the likely driver behind box and Trove prices running hot a month early. With real secondary-market data now in, Miguel's Enchanted sits at $130.68 and Hector's at $99.77, both under half of Ariel's Enchanted ($399.98) or Belle's ($292.29). The crossover got people bidding up sealed product sight-unseen; once collectors could actually pick their pulls, the chase settled on non-Coco characters instead.

Hyperia City is only two days past its wide street date. Singles and sealed volume are both still thin compared to where they'll settle over the next month, and the brand-new Iconic tier in particular has almost no sales history behind its current prices -- a handful of trades could move Mickey or Cinderella significantly in either direction. Treat everything above as an early snapshot, not a settled valuation, and check current completed listings before buying or selling into these prices.
