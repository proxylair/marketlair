Disney Lorcana's Hyperia City (Set 14) has a local-store prerelease on
October 16 and a wide retail street date of October 23 -- more than a
month out as of this writing. There are no singles on the secondary market
yet because nobody has opened a pack. What's trading right now is sealed
product only, and it's already telling a clear story about presale demand
for this one.

**Buying single packs right now costs almost double buying the box.**
Twenty-four packs at $17.49 each works out to $419.76 -- 86% more than the
$225.94 the full box is trading for. That gap is way wider than it'll be
once the set actually streets and single-pack sellers have real supply;
right now it just reflects that almost nobody's listing loose packs yet,
so the few that exist are priced like a novelty rather than 1/24th of a
box.

**The case discount is thinner than normal, which says something about
presale supply.** A box case (four boxes) is going for $854.77, or
$213.69 per box -- only about a 5% discount off buying a single box at
$225.94. Once a set is actually in stores, case buyers usually get a
meaningfully bigger break than that, because larger sellers can move
volume. A 5% case discount this early usually means distributors and
shops haven't started undercutting each other on cases yet -- everyone's
still selling off the same thin allocation.

**The Illumineer's Trove is where the real presale premium shows up.**
Ravensburger's official MSRP on the Trove is $49.99. It's currently
trading at $118.26 -- roughly 2.4x MSRP. That's a much bigger markup than
the booster box is carrying relative to its own typical retail price, and
it lines up with how Troves have behaved for past sets: they're usually a
mass-retail exclusive (Target, in past chapters), so early copies come
almost entirely from local game stores and distributors rather than big-box
allocation, which is exactly the kind of supply squeeze that inflates
presale prices. Buying the Trove by the case brings it down to $99.91 per
unit -- still double MSRP, just a smaller multiple of it.

**Part of the demand here is almost certainly the Coco crossover.** Hyperia
City is headlined by Pixar's Coco, with Miguel and Héctor Rivera getting
major treatment alongside a new "Ink drops" mechanic and a newly introduced
Illustrious rarity tier sitting above Enchanted. Lorcana has folded Pixar
properties in before, but a full Coco-centered set is new ground, and
that kind of first-time property pairing tends to pull in buyers who
don't normally chase presale sealed product -- which is consistent with
box and Trove prices both running hot a month before street date.

*All prices above are presale sealed-product snapshots taken more than a
month before Hyperia City's October 23 street date. There are no singles
on the market yet, sales volume on sealed product is still low, and every
number here -- especially the Trove and case pricing -- should be expected
to move, likely down, once prerelease events on October 16 start putting
real supply into circulation. Don't treat this as a settled price on
anything; check current completed listings before buying or selling
presale product at these levels.*
