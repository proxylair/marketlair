One Piece Card Game's eighteenth main set, "The Dominance of God" (OP-18),
doesn't officially street until November 20 -- both English and Japanese
versions release the same day. That's more than a month out as of this
writing, but TCGplayer already has presale listings live for the full set,
and the numbers are telling a very specific story: a guest-artist
collaboration, not gameplay, is driving the early money.

The headline reason to care about OP-18: Final Fantasy artist Yoshitaka
Amano painted new alternate-art illustrations for two Secret Rares in the
set. One is Loki, a reprint of OP17-119 (the same Giant/Elbaph character
that was already a $50-plus chase card from August's "World's Strongest
Warriors"), now wearing Amano's artwork instead of the original art. The
other is Saint Shamrock, a brand-new black Secret Rare Celestial Dragon
character debuting in this set. Sources covering the reveal are explicit
that this is not a Final Fantasy crossover -- it's one artist lending his
style to two One Piece cards -- and they point to Amano's earlier
alternate art for Liliana, Dreadhorde General in Magic's War of the Spark
as the closest precedent for how collectors tend to treat these.

No OP-18 product images exist yet anywhere, including on TCGplayer itself
-- that's normal for a set over a month from release -- so the gallery

card for visual reference while the table below carries the actual OP-18

**The Loki number is the one to actually sit with.** The exact same card
-- same OP17-119 print number, same stats, same character -- is trading
at $50.45 in its original OP17 printing and listed at $1,999.99 for the
Amano reprint. That's roughly a 40x gap for new art on an identical card,
and it has nothing to do with how the card plays. That's a pure
guest-artist premium, and it's the same pattern Amano's Magic alt art
drew in 2019: collectors paying for the artist's name, not the card's
power level.

**Saint Shamrock shows the same effect on a card with zero playing
history.** There's no prior printing to compare it to -- it's new to
OP-18 -- but Bandai is apparently printing it two ways: a standard black
Secret Rare at $99.99, and Amano's version at $1,999.99. A 20x spread on
a card nobody has played a single game with yet is about as clean a
reading of "guest art drives the price" as this hobby produces.

**Sealed product tells a calmer story, and it's worth doing the math
before preordering.** At $24.95 a pack, 24 packs would run $598.80 bought
individually. The box itself presales at $359.79 -- about $14.99 a pack,
a real discount for buying sealed. The case is the odd one out: $4,499.50
across 12 boxes works out to $374.96 per box, slightly more than a single
box's $359.79. Case pricing hasn't settled relative to single boxes yet,
which is common this early in a presale window before enough trades have
happened to pull the two in line.

*Every number above is a presale snapshot more than a month before
OP-18's November 20 street date, and the Amano alternate arts in
particular are trading on a handful of listings with no market-price
average available yet -- only a current low price. Treat $1,999.99 as
what's listed right now, not a settled valuation; these two cards in
particular could move sharply once real copies start shipping and pull
rates become known.*
